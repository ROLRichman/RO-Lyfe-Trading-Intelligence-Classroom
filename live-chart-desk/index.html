Check futures, economic calendar, sector posture, gaps, relative volume and the lower-priced stock watch. Build a candidate list; confirm spreads and tradability with your broker.

09:35–10:15 ETOpening momentum

Run the opening scan after the first minutes. Require a clear stock-chart entry, invalidation and volume/structure confirmation.

11:30–14:00 ETMidday trend builder

Look for pullbacks, coils, VWAP holds/reclaims and multi-day swing candidates.

After the closeReview & prepare

Update stock/option logs, market posture, lessons learned and tomorrow’s watchlist.

Times are Eastern. Pre-market trading is not available on every broker/platform, and lower-priced shares can have wider spreads, thinner liquidity and larger gap risk. A scan does not promise a trade.
