04 / LEARN THE WHY

Classroom Modules

Open a lesson, review the rule, and test your understanding.

Heikin-Ashi vs. regular candles

Heikin-Ashi smooths price presentation to make trends easier to study. Regular candles show actual OHLC prices for the period. Use Heikin-Ashi to inspect trend structure, then use actual market prices for execution and risk calculations.

Moving averages: 20 / 50 / 200

The 20-period average provides shorter-term context, the 50-period average intermediate context, and the 200-period average longer-term context. Their usefulness depends on the timeframe. A crossover is not a guarantee of future direction.

Slow Stochastic, RSI, MACD and VWAP

These tools measure different aspects of momentum or price context. Stochastic and RSI can remain overbought or oversold during strong trends. MACD can lag. VWAP is most commonly used as an intraday volume-weighted reference. Look for confluence, not certainty.

ATM options and 14–20 DTE

This is your preferred study window, not a universal best expiration. Shorter-dated options generally have greater time-decay sensitivity. Compare liquidity, spread, delta, implied volatility, event risk and the intended holding period before deciding.

Brackets, partial profits and runners

Plan the entry, invalidation, target, partial exit and runner rules before the trade. Confirm how your broker handles linked orders, stop triggers, partial fills and option order types. Stops can fill at worse prices; stop-limit orders can fail to fill.

Covered calls and calendar spreads

A covered call generally pairs 100 shares with one standard short call contract; it caps upside at the strike and still leaves substantial stock downside. A calendar spread uses different expirations at the same strike and has its own volatility, timing and expiration risks.
